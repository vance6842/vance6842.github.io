Privacy policy

vtools-bookbub is a personal script used only by its owner, on the owner's own Gmail account.

  • It reads emails from BookBub in that account and may change their labels.
  • It does not send, delete or forward email.
  • It stores only a sign-in token on the owner's own computer.
  • It shares no data with anyone.

Last updated: 30 October 2026.
